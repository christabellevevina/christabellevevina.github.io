Academic Project

Situating the Room

LocationSUTD Hostel, Singapore
ToolsRhino · Illustrator

Project Description

Situating the Room expands the project from the scale of the interior to the scale of the building. The drawing presents the SUTD hostel as the larger architectural context in which the room is located, establishing the relationship between the individual living space and its surrounding structure.

The project connects the previous floor-plan and axonometric studies by locating them within the broader hostel environment. It demonstrates how a small personal space forms part of a larger residential system and architectural composition.
